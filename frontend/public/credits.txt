Credits for the public pages of Nivas (frontend/src/pages/public/)

Pictures
  The illustrations on the front page (a hostel block with a student at the gate, and a student's
  phone) are original drawings made for this project: inline SVG in
  frontend/src/pages/public/illustrations.tsx. They are covered by the project's own licence.
  No photographs are used: free-licence photo sites (Unsplash, Pexels) block automated downloads,
  so original illustrations were used instead (decision of 11 November 2026).

App icon and favicon
  The Nivas mark (a house roof over a bed, white on the brand blue): an original drawing made for this
  project. favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png and icon-512.png are all drawn from the
  same shapes by frontend/scripts/make_icons.py.

Icons
  Lucide (https://lucide.dev), ISC licence: the npm package lucide-react, already a dependency of
  the web app.

Font
